# App Preview Video Specifications
## Camera Companion - App Store Optimization

---

## 🎬 Video Strategy Overview

### Primary Objectives
1. **Immediate Hook**: Capture attention within first 3 seconds
2. **Problem/Solution**: Show photography struggle → easy solution
3. **Core Features**: Demonstrate key functionality in action
4. **Results**: Show stunning photos achieved with the app
5. **Call-to-Action**: Drive immediate download

### Target Conversion Goals
- **Store Page Engagement**: +40% time on page
- **Install Conversion**: +25% from video viewers
- **Video Completion**: >60% completion rate
- **Click-Through**: >15% from video to download

---

## 📐 Technical Specifications

### iOS App Preview Requirements
**Video Specifications**:
- **Duration**: 15-30 seconds maximum
- **Resolution**: 
  - iPhone: 1080×1920 (portrait) or 1920×1080 (landscape)
  - iPad: 1200×1600 (portrait) or 1600×1200 (landscape)
- **Frame Rate**: 30 fps
- **Format**: .mov or .mp4
- **Codec**: H.264
- **Audio**: Optional (most users watch without sound)
- **File Size**: <500MB

**Content Requirements**:
- No text overlays or captions
- Show actual app interface
- Real device capture preferred
- No simulated interface elements
- Must represent current app version

### Android Play Store Video Requirements
**Video Specifications**:
- **Duration**: 30 seconds maximum
- **Resolution**: 
  - 16:9 landscape: 1920×1080 minimum
  - 9:16 portrait: 1080×1920 minimum
- **Frame Rate**: 30 or 60 fps
- **Format**: .mp4, .mov, .avi, .wmv, .flv, .webm
- **Audio**: Supported but optional
- **File Size**: <100MB

**Content Guidelines**:
- Text overlays allowed and recommended
- Can include marketing elements
- Voice-over permitted
- Background music allowed
- Call-to-action text encouraged

## 🎨 Visual Production Guidelines

### Filming Requirements

#### Device Capture
**Equipment Needed**:
- **Primary**: iPhone 14 Pro Max (for iOS version)
- **Secondary**: Google Pixel 7 Pro (for Android version)
- **Cameras**: Canon EOS R5, Sony α7R IV, Nikon Z9
- **Lighting**: Professional LED panels for consistent exposure
- **Tripods**: Sturdy support for both phone and cameras

**Screen Recording Setup**:
- **iOS**: Native screen recording at 1080p
- **Android**: ADB screen recording or OBS capture
- **Frame Rate**: Consistent 30fps throughout
- **Clean Interface**: Hide status bars, notifications
- **Demo Data**: Prepare realistic but perfect demo content

#### B-Roll Photography
**Scenarios to Film**:
1. **Portrait Session**: Model with professional lighting
2. **Landscape Setup**: Golden hour outdoor shooting
3. **Sports Photography**: Action sequence capture
4. **Macro Work**: Close-up flower/insect photography
5. **Studio Setup**: Professional product photography

**Camera Angles**:
- Over-shoulder perspective of photographer
- Close-up of camera controls and displays
- Wide shots establishing shooting environment
- Detail shots of connection process
- Results viewing on camera LCD

### Post-Production Workflow

#### Video Editing Guidelines
**Software Requirements**:
- **Professional**: Final Cut Pro X (Mac) or Premiere Pro
- **Audio**: Logic Pro X or Audition for sound design
- **Graphics**: After Effects for motion graphics
- **Color**: DaVinci Resolve for color grading

**Editing Principles**:
- **Pacing**: Quick but not frantic (2-3 second cuts)
- **Continuity**: Smooth transitions between scenes
- **Audio**: Consistent levels, professional mixing
- **Color**: Unified color grading across all footage
- **Graphics**: Minimal, elegant, brand-consistent

#### Motion Graphics Standards
**Typography Animation**:
- **Font**: SF Pro Display (iOS) / Roboto (Android)
- **Animation**: Subtle, professional entrance/exit
- **Timing**: Text on screen minimum 2 seconds
- **Contrast**: Always readable against background
- **Hierarchy**: Clear size/weight relationships

**UI Element Highlighting**:
- **Method**: Subtle glow or outline animation
- **Color**: Brand blue (#3b82f6) or gold (#fbbf24)
- **Duration**: 1-2 second highlight duration
- **Frequency**: Not overwhelming, strategic use
- **Purpose**: Guide viewer attention naturally

### Audio Design Strategy

#### Music Selection Criteria
**iOS Version (Background Music)**:
- **Style**: Modern, upbeat, professional
- **Instrumentation**: Electronic with organic elements
- **Energy**: Builds from calm to exciting
- **Length**: Perfect 30-second arc
- **Rights**: Royalty-free or original composition

**Android Version (Full Audio)**:
- **Voice-Over**: Professional, warm, conversational
- **Gender**: A/B test male vs female narrator
- **Accent**: Neutral American English
- **Pace**: Clear, not rushed, enthusiastic
- **Script**: Match visual timing precisely

#### Sound Effects
**Interactive Elements**:
- **App Interactions**: Subtle tap sounds, swipe whooshes
- **Camera Connections**: Satisfying connection chime
- **AI Analysis**: Gentle processing sounds
- **Photo Capture**: Realistic but enhanced shutter sound
- **Success Moments**: Positive confirmation tones

## 📊 Performance Optimization

### Video Encoding Settings

#### iOS Optimized Export
```
Format: H.264
Resolution: 1080×1920 (Portrait) / 1920×1080 (Landscape)
Frame Rate: 30 fps
Bitrate: 8-12 Mbps (VBR)
Audio: AAC, 128 kbps (if used)
Profile: High
Level: 4.0
```

#### Android Optimized Export
```
Format: H.264 (MP4 container)
Resolution: 1920×1080 (recommended)
Frame Rate: 30 fps
Bitrate: 5-8 Mbps (VBR)
Audio: AAC, 128 kbps
Profile: High
Level: 4.1
```

### Platform-Specific Optimization

#### iOS App Store Considerations
- **Auto-play**: Videos auto-play without sound
- **Loop**: Seamless loop for continuous viewing
- **Thumbnail**: First frame as video thumbnail
- **Loading**: Optimize for quick loading on cellular
- **Quality**: Maintain quality while minimizing file size

#### Google Play Store Considerations
- **Captions**: Include closed captions for accessibility
- **Multiple Languages**: Create localized versions
- **Thumbnail**: Custom thumbnail selection allowed
- **Analytics**: Track engagement metrics
- **Updates**: Easy to update without app submission
